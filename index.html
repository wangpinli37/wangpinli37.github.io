From Objectives to What Models Learn: A Landau Theory of Invariant Learning

Pinli Wang, Yue He, Peng Cui

A statistical-physics framework for understanding invariant learning. Low-order signatures of learning objectives predict phase boundaries, representation loadings, and when regularization selectively removes shortcut modes while retaining stable ones.

Research project

Robust Biomarker Discovery via Adversarial De-confounding

Developing domain-generalization methods to separate disease-related signals from clinical confounders in high-dimensional proteomics. This work identifies limitations of Lasso and discovers more effective biomarkers.
